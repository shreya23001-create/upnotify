OpenAI vs Anthropic Uptime — Week of 18 April 2026

If you're building on top of an AI API, reliability isn't just a nice-to-have — it directly affects your users. Every week, Uptrue's monitoring infrastructure runs independent HTTP checks every 5 minutes across the leading AI providers. This week we're looking at OpenAI and Anthropic head to head.

TL;DR
  • OpenAI edged out Anthropic on uptime: 99.94% vs 99.91%
  • Anthropic was 25ms faster on average response time (287ms vs 312ms)
  • OpenAI had 1 incident (18 min); Anthropic had 2 incidents totalling 42 min
  • Neither provider had a major outage — both performed well this week

Uptime This Week

Both providers maintained strong availability this week. OpenAI's single incident on Tuesday (18 minutes, 06:12–06:30 UTC) was caught by Uptrue's two-confirmation system and logged as a confirmed outage. Anthropic experienced two shorter interruptions on Monday and Thursday.

A difference of 0.03 percentage points sounds small, but at scale it translates to roughly 4 minutes of additional downtime per week for Anthropic users. For AI-powered applications with SLA commitments, that gap matters. Both providers remain within what most teams would consider "four nines" territory.

Response Time

Response time tells a different story. Anthropic's Claude API endpoints responded consistently faster this week, with a median of 287ms compared to OpenAI's 312ms. This is the time from request initiation to first byte — not including model inference time — so it primarily reflects infrastructure latency and routing efficiency.

If your application is latency-sensitive — for example, streaming completions in a real-time chat interface — Anthropic's infrastructure edge may be worth factoring into your provider decision.

OpenAI — Better for

About This Data

All uptime, response time, and incident data in this report is collected exclusively by Uptrue's independent monitoring infrastructure. We perform HTTP checks every 5 minutes from monitoring nodes and run keyword detection against each provider's official status page every 15 minutes.

An incident is recorded only after 2 consecutive failed checks from the same node, eliminating single-packet-loss false positives. Uptrue is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any of the services monitored here. Data represents external availability as experienced from our monitoring nodes and may differ from internal metrics reported by the providers themselves.
